About Us

Where Precision Meets Relaxation

For over 8 years, Dreamy Claws Nail Studio has been Austin's destination for luxury nail care. Founded on the belief that a manicure should feel as good as it looks, our team blends advanced technique — from Russian manicures to BIAB overlays — with an unhurried, spa-like atmosphere.

FAQs

Common Questions

How much does a gel manicure cost in Austin? +
A classic gel manicure at Dreamy Claws starts at $45 and takes about 45 minutes. Pricing increases slightly for extended length, intricate art, or French tips.
How long do Gel-X extensions last? +
Gel-X extensions typically last 3 to 4 weeks before a fill is needed, and can last even longer with proper aftercare and cuticle oil use.
What is the difference between BIAB and acrylic nails? +
BIAB (Builder in a Bottle) is a flexible gel-based overlay that strengthens natural nails with a lightweight, glass-like finish, while acrylic is a firmer powder-and-liquid system best suited for adding length and structural extensions.
Is a Russian manicure safe? +
Yes, when performed by a trained technician with sterilized e-file bits. A Russian manicure uses precise electric-file work to remove cuticle tissue for an ultra-clean, long-lasting result, and our artists are certified in this dry-manicure technique.
Do you accept walk-ins? +
Walk-ins are welcome based on availability, but we recommend booking online in advance, especially on weekends and before holidays, to guarantee your preferred time and artist.
How far in advance should I book bridal nails? +
We recommend booking bridal and bridal party appointments 4 to 6 weeks ahead, with a trial appointment 2 to 3 weeks before the wedding to finalize your design.
How do you sterilize your tools? +
All metal tools are cleaned and processed in a hospital-grade autoclave after every client, and all files, buffers, and toe separators are single-use or fully disposed of between clients.
What nail shape is right for me? +
Shorter, rounder shapes like oval and squoval suit everyday wear and shorter nail beds, while almond, coffin, and stiletto shapes elongate the fingers and work well with extensions. Our shape guide and your nail artist can help you choose during your visit.
